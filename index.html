The fixed design style

Marketplace Infographic — the Wildberries / Ozon product-card look, the same family as Amazon A+ feature-callout modules, built on an Apple-style bento grid with oversized editorial typography.

Non-negotiable rules in every prompt

  • block No face, head, hair or full body — hands or forearms only
  • center_focus_strong Product is always the hero: large, centred, tack-sharp
  • blur_on Background softly blurred, one or two quiet props at most
  • lock Product lock — colour, design, print and size never change

Design language written into every infographic

  • grid_view Bento grid of rounded panels, generous white space
  • title One oversized bold display headline with tight tracking
  • palette Monochrome palette derived from the product colour
  • my_location Macro feature callouts with thin leader lines and pill badge

FAQ

    

      
Will anyone’s face appear in the images?expand_more
No. Every prompt carries a strict no-face rule — no face, head, hair or body, only hands or forearms cropped at the wrist. The product always stays the hero: large, centred, sharp, with a blurred background. No models needed and no likeness issues.

      
Does this page create images or prompts?expand_more
Prompts only, every time. It never calls an image model, so quota, 429 and “limit 0” errors can never appear. Copy a prompt into Google AI Studio and the image is generated there on your existing plan.

      
What is this photo style called?expand_more
Marketplace Infographic — in Russia it is called “инфографика для маркетплейсов”, the Wildberries and Ozon product-card design. On Amazon the equivalent is A+ Content or EBC infographics with feature callouts. The layout is a bento grid, the large lettering is oversized display typography, and single-colour shading is a monochromatic palette.

      
How do I paste a prompt in AI Studio?expand_more
Press Copy on any prompt, open Google AI Studio, use the plus button to upload your product photo, paste the prompt and press enter. Download the result and upload it to Meesho.

      
What if the product colour or design changes?expand_more
It will not. A product lock clause is appended to every prompt. If a model still drifts, add “keep the product exactly as in the uploaded photo” at the end and run it again.

      
How does it pick the trend?expand_more
It reads today’s date and works out the current and upcoming Indian festivals and season — Raksha Bandhan, Independence Day, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Diwali, wedding season, monsoon, summer, back to school — then writes the scene, props and banner copy to match.
