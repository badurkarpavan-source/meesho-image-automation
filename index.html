Sawaal jawaab

  
 

  
Kya ye bilkul free hai?
Haan. Page free hai aur tumhari Google AI Studio ki free key se images banti hain. Koi subscription nahi.

  
Images kahan save hoti hain?
Har image ke niche ⬇️ Download button hai — file tumhare phone/laptop ke Downloads folder me jaati hai. Kisi server pe kuch save nahi hota.

  
Meri key safe hai?
Key sirf tumhare browser me (localStorage) rehti hai aur seedha Google ko jaati hai. Kisi ko share na karo; shak ho to AI Studio se delete karke nayi bana lo.

  
Meesho ke liye kaunsi image main lagani chahiye?
"Main image (white bg)" wali — Meesho pure white background maangta hai. Baaki images gallery me lagao.

  
Ek image galat aa gayi to?
Dobara "Images Banao" daba do, ya features me thoda साफ likho. Har baar naya result aata hai.

  
Wholesaler ki photo use kar sakta hoon?
Haan, reference ke liye — AI usse naya photo banata hai. Par doosre ke logo/watermark wali photo sidha listing me na lagao.
